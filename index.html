What StudyPace learned

—

Every finished focus session is compared with what you planned. After five sessions of one kind, StudyPace starts showing you an adjusted range instead of the one you picked.

How your own estimates behave

What this changes

You picked 2–4 h for lab reports. Your own sessions run about 1.6× longer, so the weekly meter now counts them as 3–6 h. That is why this week reads 11–17 h and not 9–14 h.

Research on the planning fallacy says students underestimate how long work takes, and the interviews said the same. This is the one place where the app can correct for it with your own data instead of a general rule.

Tap a week to open it into days.

Light is under 10 hours, Balanced is 10 to 18, Overloaded is above your weekly limit of 18 hours. The upper number of each range decides the label.

How long will this take?

Three students in the Assignment 2 interviews said an exact number of hours would always be wrong. A CHECK constraint in the database makes a single exact value impossible to store.
